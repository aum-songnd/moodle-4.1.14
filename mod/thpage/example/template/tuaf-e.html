2. COURSE DESCRIPTION

English 3 is a mandatory basic course in a series of 4 English courses in the bachelor training program of Food Technology. The course provides learners with an overall understanding of academic IELTS reading paper format and different types of test questions. Moreover, students will apply fundamental test-taking skills and strategies in IELTS reading papers into doing IELTS reading tests. A variety of practice tests are supplied for students’ improvement of reading proficiency.

3. COURSE OBJECTIVE

By the end of the course, learners will be able to gain IELTS reading scores 5.0 -5.5. Specifically, they are expected to:

- Describe academic IELTS reading paper structure and question types in IELTS reading paper;

- Apply test-taking skills to answer each question type in IELTS readings, expand vocabulary, develop their language proficiency as well as practice strategies in academic IELTS readings papers;

- Self-study and do their own research for English learning to reach the objectives of the course.

8. COURSE REQUIREMENT

8.1. STUDENT’S REQUIREMENT

Students need to clearly understand the learning content, study plan, and final exam plan;

  • Compulsory learning tasks:

Attend classes: Fully participate in the learning weeks on electronic data sets;

Exercises: Complete all required exercises;

Q&A - discussion: Actively participate in Q&A and discussions on the forum, including: Situations given by the lecturer; Create their own topics and participate in discussion of topics on the forum.

  • Scientific ethics:

The exercises done by students, if found to be copied from each other or from Internet sources, will be deducted 100% of that lesson; if it is at a serious level (for many people to copy, >= 3 students do the same test) will be banned from taking the final exam for both students who copy and students who let others copy.

Students who fail to complete the tasks under Section 8.3 will be banned from exam;

Students who take the exam for someone else or students who use an examiner on their behalf will be suspended or expelled from university.

8.2. COURSE COMPLETION CONDITIONS

Conditions for taking the final exam:

Do not violate scientific ethics (section 8.1)

Attendance score: Equal to or greater than 5 points;

Process test score: Equal to or greater than 5 points;

  • Course completion condition:

Overall score: Equal to or greater than 4/10 points (~ grade D).

  • Students must retake the test in the following situations:

Students who are absent from the exam due to: illness or other special reasons must be certified by the competent authority, the University,

  • Students must restudy the course in the following situations:

Students are not eligible to take the final exam;

The final grade of the course is less than 4/10 points.

Students are eligible to take the final exam but the final exam score = 0.
